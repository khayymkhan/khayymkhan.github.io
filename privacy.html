Privacy Policy

Sharp Words · by Smart Ideas Studio

Sharp Words is made by Smart Ideas Studio (Muhammad Asif), Pakistan, who is responsible for this app. This policy explains what the app does with information. Last updated: 25 October 2026.

What we collect

We collect nothing ourselves. Sharp Words has no accounts and no servers of its own, and we never see or store your personal information. The app does show ads from Google, and Google may collect some information to do that (see Advertising below).

What stays on your phone

Your progress (points, level, streak, badges, solved sayings), your settings, the optional name you add to certificates, and your saved sayings are stored only on your phone. If Google backup is switched on in your phone's settings, Android may include this progress in your own private Google backup so it can come back on a new phone. We cannot see that backup.

Advertising

Sharp Words is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner on some screens, a full-screen ad now and then between puzzles, and short videos only when you choose one for a bonus. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland you are asked for your consent first, and you can choose non-personalised ads. In US states with privacy laws you can opt out of personalised ads. Google explains how it uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

You can change your ad choices at any time in the app under Settings → Your progress → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Remove Ads

You can pay once to remove ads. The payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We never see your card or payment details, only confirmation that the purchase was made, which is kept on your phone.

Reminders

Daily and evening reminders are scheduled on your phone. No information is sent anywhere to make them work.

Sharing, saving and email

When you choose Share, Save or Send feedback, the app hands a picture or text to the app you pick (for example a messaging or email app). What happens next is covered by that app's privacy policy. If you email us, we use your message only to reply.

Ratings

If the app was installed from Google Play, the rating card is provided by Google Play.

Permissions

Internet (for ads and purchases), the advertising ID (for ads), notifications (for reminders), vibration, and checking whether you're online. The app does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or EEA you have rights over your personal data, such as access and deletion. Because we hold none ourselves, requests about ad data are handled by Google; you can also write to us and we will help.

Children

Sharp Words is suitable for everyone but is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If anything changes, this page will be updated before the new version of the app is released, and the date above will change.

Contact
